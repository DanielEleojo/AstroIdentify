Project Overview

AstroIdentify is an AI-powered constellation classification system that uses deep learning to identify constellations from astronomical images. Built with state-of-the-art computer vision techniques, it can accurately classify 12 different constellations.

Whether you're an astronomy enthusiast, student, or professional, AstroIdentify makes constellation identification accessible and instant. Simply upload an image, and our trained neural network will analyze the star patterns and provide you with accurate classification results.
